Questions before you call?

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! Zent Family Dentistry is welcoming new patients of all ages. Call (574) 234-4117 to make an appointment.

Do you take my dental insurance?

We accept almost all dental insurance plans and are in-network with Cigna. The only plans we can't accept are Medicaid and DMO/HMO plans. We'll file your claims for you — call and our front desk can check your plan.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

Ask about our VIP Smile Club in-house membership plan. We also offer financing through CareCredit, Sunbit and Cherry.

Is there a dentist near me in Mishawaka or elsewhere in Michiana?

Our office at 3410 Douglas Rd in South Bend sits between SR-23 and Grape Road, making it convenient for patients from Mishawaka, Granger, Niles and across Michiana.

Do you see kids and the whole family?

Yes — we're a family dentistry practice, so parents, kids and grandparents can all be seen at one dental office.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 8am to 5pm.

What happens at my first visit?

A comprehensive exam, digital X-rays, a professional cleaning and an oral cancer screening, plus a clear explanation of anything we find and your options.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Our team takes the time to explain every step and works at your pace so you feel comfortable throughout your visit.
